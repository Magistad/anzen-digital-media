The alarm goes off and you still have crates on the floor. That is the moment this game is actually about. Not the gunfight. Whether you stay and finish the room or take what you already bagged and leave.

I treat it as a simple call. Once the loot on you is worth more than what is left in the room, I go. The rest is a bonus I only chase if the street still feels quiet.

You cannot see how many people are in the lobby. Rob Run does not show you a player count. You have to listen. Gunfire close by, cars stacking up, players crossing your path. Quiet, normal, or packed. That is the whole census.

Hotspots get more of that traffic. A corner store on a dead street is not the same as a marked building everyone farms. If you have been jumped at the same place three runs in a row, believe that, not a feeling that this time will be different.

Wanted heat is the other clock. In the build I have been playing, staying stealthy keeps you at 0. Killing civilians bumps it. At 3 you are Most Wanted and they can see you on the map. I try to leave before I get there. I have not rechecked this against the current patch, so do not treat those numbers as locked. This draft stays marked until I do.

Jail time is the part people shrug off until it happens. The version I saw added a year per $500,000 in assets, capped at 40 years around $20 million, plus more if you are carrying Lenny's debt. Same warning: I need to verify that before this is a real guide. If it is even close, a fat bag is also a longer sentence, which is another reason not to get greedy after the alarm.

The exit still has to happen. You have to get to the vehicle, reach a stash, and unload. A packed street makes that slower and more likely to go wrong. If the room is already loud, I would rather leave with a half bag than drop the whole run outside the door.
