You do not need a stack of new products to get something useful from AI. Most small businesses get the value from four jobs: a first draft, a summary, a cleanup of a spreadsheet, and a transcript of a meeting you were already in.

## A first draft

A general chat tool can write a rough email, a product description, or a job post. You still check the facts and the tone. Do not send the draft to a customer without reading it. The tool does not know your prices, your promises, or what you are allowed to say.

## A summary

Paste a long document you have the right to use, and ask for the decisions, the dates, and the open questions. Use the summary as a map back to the original. Do not treat it as a replacement for a contract.

## A spreadsheet

Ask how to write a formula, or ask the tool to point at rows that do not match. Keep the file on your own computer. Check the formula against a row you can do by hand.

## A transcript

A transcription tool helps after a meeting of your own team, or after a conversation where the other person agreed to be recorded. Read it before you share it. Names and numbers are where these tools slip.

If the job is remembering a closing date, a chat transcript is a weak system of record. A dated list you control is a better one. [Closepath](/products/closepath) is Anzen's listing-to-close book for solo residential agents. It is a product, separate from these notes.

## What not to paste

Passwords, bank logins, full card numbers, unpublished financials, and anything a client gave you under a duty to keep quiet. A consumer chat box is not a filing cabinet. If the settings let you turn off training on your chats, turn that off before you paste anything sensitive. If you cannot tell what the company keeps, do not paste it.

## Pick one tool

Choose a tool with a price you can see, a way to export or delete your data, and a business plan if customer information will go into it. Run one real task for a week. If it does not save time on that task, cancel it. A "best tools" list from last year is not a plan, because the features move.
