Anzen Digital Media LLC is a Nevada company. I file the annual list myself. This is the version I wish someone had handed me the first year, with the statute linked, not a blog's summary of the statute.

It is general information. It is not legal advice about your entity. The fee in your ORION cart beats anything I type here.

## Last day of the anniversary month

[NRS 86.263](https://www.leg.state.nv.us/nrs/NRS-086.html#NRS086Sec263) puts a Nevada LLC's annual list due on or before the last day of the month the state formed the company. Formed any day in March, you file by March 31. The state business license renewal rides with that list.

A company formed somewhere else and registered in Nevada uses the registration anniversary, not the day you felt like a Nevada business.

The Secretary of State can send a reminder about 90 days out. Missing that email does not move the date. I put the last day of the anniversary month on my own calendar and ignore the hope that a notice will save me.

Filing early can miss the year you meant. The same statute says a list filed more than 90 days before it is due can be treated as an amended list for the prior year. If you are tempted to knock it out in January for a September deadline, read the section or ask the Secretary of State before you pay.

## What I pay the state for an LLC

On the current fee schedule, a typical Nevada LLC pays $150 for the annual list and $200 to renew the state business license. That is $350, paid to the State of Nevada. The forms and fees live at [nvsos.gov](https://www.nvsos.gov/sos/businesses/commercial-recordings/forms-fees).

Do not assume that total if you are a corporation or some other entity. The business license fee is not one number for every filing. Late penalties are real. The amount in the portal is the amount.

## ORION, and why I will not log in

File at [orion.nv.gov](https://orion.nv.gov/). ORION replaced SilverFlume. My old SilverFlume login is what I try first. If yours fails, use the Secretary of State's help, not a stranger's offer to "just hop in the account."

Have the entity name, the Nevada entity number if you know it, and the names and addresses of the managers or managing members.

The [Nevada annual list filing kit](/services/nevada-annual-compliance-kit) is $99 to me. It is a checklist in the order the portal asks, filled with the details you send. You submit it. The $350, when that is your figure, never touches my Stripe account.
